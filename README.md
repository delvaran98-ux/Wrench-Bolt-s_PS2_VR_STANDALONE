The APKs bundle GTA III game data and other media. That content remains copyrighted by its respective rights holders. Share these builds only with people who have the right to use the game, and do not make the Drive files or repository public unless you have permission to distribute all bundled content.

These VR PORT Projects are not for any gain, the only goal is to preserve older titles that will fade with digital media evolution.

Please respect the original creators as well, With the new rise of technology and AI any game is possible to port in anyway.
My goal is not to focus on new titles, or divert attention away from Game Devs of recent titles.
I only focus on older ones that are fading away and will be preserved in this way for people to re-experience in VR.

Hope this brings happiness to some people come support me if you want at Nostalgic Dragon VR on YOUTUBE.
